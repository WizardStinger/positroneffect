Five stages, in order.

Each stage hands a locked result to the next, so late edits never undo early decisions.

STAGE 1 · IDEA

Bring the script

Paste your own text or give a topic and let the writer draft it. Choose a visual style and a platform format.

STAGE 2 · CAST

Pick the narrator and characters

Choose a voice and a look, or upload a reference photo on higher plans. The cast is fixed for the whole film.

STAGE 3 · SCENES

Shape the storyboard

Reorder, split, merge or delete scenes until the pacing feels right. Narration timing updates as you go.

STAGE 4 · POLISH

Refine on the timeline

Swap a shot, retime a line, change the score or redo the thumbnail without regenerating the rest.

STAGE 5 · RELEASE

Export and publish

Download in the right shape for each platform and label it as AI-generated in one click.

Questions, answered.

Anything missing? Ask in the community channel.

What is Positron Effect?

A platform that produces long videos from a script. It writes or accepts the script, generates scenes and narration, adds a score and builds a thumbnail, then lets you edit everything from one timeline.

What kinds of videos can I make?

Explainers, stories, lessons and marketing pieces in photoreal, cinematic, anime, cartoon, painted and line-art styles, formatted for YouTube, TikTok, Instagram and LinkedIn.

How long can a film be?

From 30 seconds up to 45 minutes depending on plan. The free credit covers a 45 second film.

Do I need editing experience?

No. Describe a change in a sentence and the scene updates. The timeline is there when you want finer control.

Is the output labelled as AI?

Voices, visuals and characters are synthetic. Export includes an optional label so you can disclose it wherever you publish.
